How this hub works

Every Monday morning the hub automatically collects the past week’s updates from official sources (StudentAid.gov, the Federal Register, CFPB, FTC, the Federal Reserve, FDIC), financial-education organizations (Jump$tart, Council for Economic Education, EVERFI, MyMoney.gov), consumer advocates (National Consumer Law Center, TICAS), and U.S. news coverage.

Each item is sorted into a topic by keywords. Items that mention deadlines, new rules or effective dates get a Heads up badge.

  • Official government agencies and established nonprofits.
  • News news articles found via Google News. Useful for context, but confirm details with an official source.

Adding your expertise

Staff notes and teaching tips live in one simple file, config/staff_notes.yaml.

  1. On any news item, click ＋ Add staff note. A ready-made snippet is copied to your clipboard.
  2. {% if edit_notes_url %}Open the staff notes file{% else %}Open config/staff_notes.yaml on GitHub{% endif %} and click the pencil (edit) icon.
  3. Paste the snippet under notes:, write your note, and click Commit changes.
  4. The site rebuilds in about a minute, and your note appears on the item (pinned notes go to the top of the home page).

Need an update right now?

{% if run_url %}Open the update workflow{% else %}In GitHub, open Actions → Weekly update{% endif %}, click Run workflow, and the site refreshes in a few minutes.

Important

This hub gathers headlines automatically and does not give advice. Always confirm details on the official source before advising a client.
