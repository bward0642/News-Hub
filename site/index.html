💡 Teaching tips

Check loan status before every repayment conversation

Ask clients to sign in at StudentAid.gov and pull up "My Aid" during the session. Loan type, servicer, and repayment plan drive almost every recommendation.

Free credit reports, every week

All three bureaus offer free weekly reports at AnnualCreditReport.com — the only official site. A great live demo for credit workshops.

Use the Loan Simulator for "what if" planning

The federal Loan Simulator compares repayment plans using the client's real loans. Walk through it together rather than estimating by hand.
