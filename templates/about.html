How this hub works

Every Monday morning the hub automatically collects the past week’s updates from official sources (StudentAid.gov, the Federal Register, CFPB, FTC, the Federal Reserve, FDIC), financial-education organizations (Jump$tart, Council for Economic Education, EVERFI, MyMoney.gov), consumer advocates (National Consumer Law Center, TICAS), and U.S. news coverage.

Each item is sorted into a topic by keywords. Items that mention deadlines, new rules or effective dates get a Heads up badge.

  • Official government agencies and established nonprofits.
  • News news articles found via Google News. Useful for context, but confirm details with an official source.

Adding your expertise

Staff can share what a story means for our clients, or pass along a teaching tip. No account or special access is needed.

  1. Click ＋ Add staff note under any story. A short form opens with the story already filled in.{% if tip_form %} For a general tip, use Suggest a teaching tip.{% endif %}
  2. Write your note, add your name, and submit.
  3. An admin reviews it. Once approved, it appears on the site within about an hour.

Please don’t include client names or other personal details. Notes are visible to anyone who has the site’s link.

Who can change the site?

Only site admins can change the website’s design, news sources, topics and settings. Staff contribute through the note form, and every submission is reviewed before it is published.
